Hospital Overview

KLE Hospital & Medical Research Centre

Located at Gabbur Cross, Hubballi – 580028, Karnataka, and attached to KLE JGMM Medical College. The campus spans 25 acres with a planned 1,366-bed capacity, offering advanced multidisciplinary clinical services and community healthcare initiatives.

Overview

Integrated Medical Education & Healthcare Campus

Associated with KLE JGMM Medical College, our Hubballi facility unites tertiary patient care, medical training, diagnostics, and clinical research under one umbrella.

Key milestones include the launch of the Free Medical Camp OPD on January 1, 2025, followed by the 24/7 Accident & Emergency Department on March 6, 2025, and expanded outpatient care services later that spring.
